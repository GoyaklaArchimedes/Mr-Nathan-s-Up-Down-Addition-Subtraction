**Mr Nathan's Up-Down Addition & Down-Up Subtraction** is a complete interactive mental arithmetic system for children aged 5–7. It teaches children to add and subtract 9, 8, 7 and 6 — the four near-ten numbers that cause the most difficulty at primary level — quickly, confidently, and entirely in their head.

The product combines two companion methods into one seamless app:

- **Up-Down Addition** — adding 9, 8, 7 or 6 to any number by rounding up to the next ten and adjusting
- **Down-Up Subtraction** — subtracting 9, 8, 7 or 6 from any number using the exact reverse process

Once a child understands that these two operations are mirror images of each other, mental arithmetic with near-ten numbers becomes fast, reliable and automatic.

---

## The Methods

### Up-Down Addition

When you add 9, it needs just 1 more to become 10. So that 1 comes from the number being added to — which goes down by 1 while the 9 goes up to 10.

- **9 + 5**: 9 goes UP to 10 (+1), 5 goes DOWN to 4 (−1) → answer: **14**
- **8 + 5**: 8 goes UP to 10 (+2), 5 goes DOWN to 3 (−2) → answer: **13**
- **7 + 5**: 7 goes UP to 10 (+3), 5 goes DOWN to 2 (−3) → answer: **12**
- **6 + 5**: 6 goes UP to 10 (+4), 5 goes DOWN to 1 (−4) → answer: **11**

### Down-Up Subtraction

Subtracting 9 is the same as subtracting 10 and adding 1 back. The number being subtracted from is split into its two digits — the tens digit goes Down by 1 and the ones digit goes Up by the complement.

- **34 − 9**: tens 3→2 (−1), ones 4→5 (+1) → answer: **25**
- **35 − 8**: tens 3→2 (−1), ones 5→7 (+2) → answer: **27**
- **46 − 7**: tens 4→3 (−1), ones 6→9 (+3) → answer: **39**
- **45 − 6**: tens 4→3 (−1), ones 5→9 (+4) → answer: **39**

### The Smart Choice

For larger numbers, both the number being added and the number being added to may have near-ten digits. The app teaches children to look at both numbers and choose the most efficient one to round up — the one closest to the next ten. This builds genuine mathematical thinking, not just mechanical rule-following.

## What's Inside

### Addition — Three Stages

**Stage 1 — Ball Animation**
Two strips of billiard-style balls. Balls arc smoothly from the bottom strip to the top strip, the top number becomes a round ten, and the badges update. Slow, visual and unmistakable.

**Stage 2 — Number Animation**
Two large number boxes. Press Show Me and the numbers roll like a slot machine — the efficient number spins up to the next ten while the other spins down by the same amount. The Up-Down movement is literally visible.

**Stage 3 — Up-Down Digits**
The answer's own digits are shown in two boxes. The tens digit counts up, the ones digit counts down — building the habit of seeing the answer forming directly from the numbers.

### Subtraction — Digit Box Animation
The number being subtracted from is split into its two digits in large separate boxes. Press Show Me and the tens digit rolls down while the ones digit rolls up. Coral indicators show exactly what changes and why.

### Graduated Learning
- **Questions 1 & 2**: guided — the animation plays automatically so the child watches first
- **Questions 3 onwards**: independent — the answer box is active immediately. The child types straight away. A hint button is available if needed. Getting it right without a hint earns extra praise.

### Worked Example Card
Every learning page shows a fixed worked example at the top — a static before-and-after snapshot the child can glance at before attempting the question. Always visible, never intrusive.

### Smart Choice (Double Digit and Double+Double levels)
For larger number questions, the two numbers are tappable. The student chooses which is more efficient to round up. A correct choice is praised. An incorrect choice is gently redirected with an explanation, then the correct animation plays anyway.

### Flash Test
The question flashes on screen for 1, 2 or 3 seconds (child's choice), then disappears. The child types the full answer from memory. Ten questions, scored out of 10. Two attempts per question — a wrong answer on the second attempt reveals the correct answer and moves on. No wrong answer is ever marked correct.

### Progression System
Addition: +9 unlocks first, then +8, +7, +6, Double Digit, Double+Double — each unlocked by completing the previous Flash Test.

Subtraction: locked until all addition levels are complete. Completing Up-Down Addition unlocks Down-Up Subtraction as a reward.

Subtraction: −9 unlocks first, then −8, −7, −6, Teens (12−19).

## Design

- Ages 5–7 primarily, also suitable for older children with learning difficulties
- Deep purple starfield background
- Gold/yellow colour identity for addition, coral/red for subtraction
- Fredoka One display font — large, bold, friendly
- Large tap targets, big text, minimal clutter
- No ads, no tracking, no login required

---

## How to Use

The app is a single HTML file. No installation required. Open it in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or interactive whiteboard. Works offline once downloaded.

- **At home**: parent and child work through it together
- **In the classroom**: display on a whiteboard, lead the class through each stage
- **Independent practice**: children work through it alone once they know how
- **Intervention**: use specific levels to target exactly the number combinations a child is struggling with
